              Short clips for editing into a promotional video

HOW TO USE THIS FILE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) and start a new chat.
2. Copy one prompt below at a time and paste it in. Veo generates ~8-second
   clips per generation.
3. Generate 2-3 variations of each scene and pick the best takes.
4. Download the clips, then edit them together in CapCut / Premiere /
   DaVinci Resolve. Add your own text overlays, logo, voiceover, and music.

TIPS BEFORE YOU START
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- All prompts are written for CINEMATIC, REALISTIC footage with NO TEXT in
  the shot. Add "KaisySales" text overlays yourself during editing so the
  AI doesn't garble the spelling.
- Keep the brand look: warm golden light, earthy tones (terracotta red-
  brown, forest green, cream), real African entrepreneurs, modern + hopeful
  mood.
- If a clip looks too dark or too fast, add: "soft natural lighting,
  slow smooth motion" to the end of the prompt.

--- SCENE 1: THE HERO SHOT (opening clip) -------------------------------------
A confident young Ghanaian entrepreneur standing on a rooftop overlooking
Accra at golden hour, holding a smartphone, smiling as she looks at the
screen. Warm sunset light, cinematic depth of field, slow push-in camera
movement from wide shot to medium close-up. She wears smart casual clothing
with terracotta and earth tones. Background city softly blurred. Hopeful,
inspiring mood. Photorealistic, shallow depth of field, 4K quality.

--- SCENE 2: THE OLD WAY (the problem) ----------------------------------------
Close-up of a frustrated small business owner sitting at a cluttered desk in
a small shop, flipping through a worn paper notebook full of messy numbers,
shaking his head slightly. Dim warm indoor lighting, realistic textures of
paper and wood desk. Slow lateral tracking shot. Moody but not depressing -
this is the "before" moment. Photorealistic, cinematic color grading.

--- SCENE 3: THE SWITCH (light bulb moment) -----------------------------------
The same small business owner picks up his smartphone and his face softens
into relief and a smile as he sees something clear and organized on screen.
Screen light gently illuminating his face, warm interior lighting, slow
zoom-in on his face from over-the-shoulder angle. Transition from confusion
to confidence. Photorealistic, warm cinematic tones, emotional and uplifting.

--- SCENE 4: THE APP IN HAND (product moment) ---------------------------------
Extreme close-up over-the-shoulder shot of hands scrolling smoothly through
a clean business dashboard app on a smartphone. The screen shows simple
charts and numbers glowing softly (abstract UI, no readable text). Cream and
terracotta colored interface hints. Soft window light, blurred cozy shop
background, macro lens feel, shallow depth of field, smooth slow scroll
motion. Premium tech commercial aesthetic, photorealistic.

--- SCENE 5: THE FREELANCER (gig economy) -------------------------------------
A young freelance designer working on a laptop in a bright modern home
office with plants, wearing headphones around her neck. She finishes typing,
leans back and smiles while checking her phone showing a payment
notification style glow. Golden afternoon light through window, gentle
camera dolly from left to right, creative workspace with sketches and
laptop. Upbeat, modern, aspirational energy. Photorealistic, cinematic.

--- SCENE 6: THE MARKET VENDOR (retail life) ----------------------------------
A cheerful middle-aged woman selling colorful fabrics in a vibrant African
market stall, serving a customer, then quickly tapping her phone to record
the sale and giving a satisfied nod. Bustling market background slightly
blurred with rich colors, natural daylight, handheld documentary-style
camera with subtle movement. Authentic, energetic, joyful atmosphere.
Photorealistic, vivid but natural colors.

--- SCENE 7: THE INVOICE MOMENT ------------------------------------------------
Close-up of a professional invoice document sliding across a wooden table
toward the camera, clean white paper with subtle terracotta design elements
at the top edge, then a hand stamps it gently with approval gesture in the
background bokeh. Studio-quality product lighting, slow elegant camera
push-in, crisp details, premium minimalist commercial style. Satisfying and
professional mood.

--- SCENE 8: GROWTH VISUAL (abstract) ------------------------------------------
Cinematic abstract animation of a green plant sprout growing in fast
timelapse from rich terracotta-brown soil, transforming into rising glowing
green bar chart shapes made of light particles ascending upward against a
warm cream background. Smooth camera tilt-up following the growth. Elegant
motion graphics style mixed with photorealistic soil and leaf textures,
soft studio lighting, inspiring and premium fintech brand aesthetic.

--- SCENE 9: TEAM / CUSTOMER LOVE (testimonial b-roll) --------------------------
Portrait-style shot of three different happy African business owners (a
shopkeeper, a barber, an online seller) each briefly looking at camera and
smiling warmly in their workplaces, quick elegant cuts between them, each
in their authentic environment with warm natural light. Gentle handheld
camera, genuine candid laughter, documentary realism, heartwarming
community feeling. Photorealistic.

--- SCENE 10: THE END CARD BACKGROUND (for your logo/text overlay) -------------
Slow elegant cinematic shot of a calm minimal desk scene at dusk: a
smartphone lying flat next to a small potted green plant and a notebook,
soft warm lamplight with gentle bokeh lights in background, terracotta and
cream color palette, very slow drifting camera movement, lots of empty
space in upper half of frame for text overlay. Peaceful premium closing
shot, photorealistic, shallow depth of field.
