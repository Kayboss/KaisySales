Why is it free?

KaisySales is in its growth phase and the priority is getting the product in front of real businesses, not billing them. Every feature is open while that continues, and you will be told before anything changes.

What happens to my data if pricing changes?

Nothing is deleted. Your records belong to you, they stay in your account, and you can export or ask for them at any time. See the Privacy Policy for detail on how data is stored and protected.
