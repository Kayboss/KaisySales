1. ABOUT THE COMPANY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KaisySales is a digital financial management platform built to help small
businesses, freelancers, and modern entrepreneurs take full control of their
money. From recording daily sales and tracking expenses to managing inventory,
generating professional invoices, and understanding business performance
through reports and analytics, KaisySales acts as an all-in-one digital
financial partner.

Born out of a simple observation — that too many promising businesses fail not
because of poor products or services, but because of poor financial visibility
— KaisySales replaces the notebooks, scattered spreadsheets, and guesswork
with one clean, easy-to-use platform accessible from any device with a browser.

What KaisySales does:
  - Sales & Revenue Tracking: Record every transaction, view daily summaries,
    and monitor cash flow in real time.
  - Expense Management: Log every cost, from stock purchases to subscriptions,
    and know exactly where the money goes.
  - Inventory & Stock Control: Track stock levels with low-stock alerts.
  - Professional Invoicing: Create, print, and download branded invoices,
    including milestone-based service invoices.
  - Projects & Services Suite: Track income per milestone, automatically split
    platform fees (Upwork, Fiverr, Freelancer), manage recurring retainers,
    and see net earnings after fees.
  - Reports & Analytics: Interactive charts (cash flow trends, revenue by
    platform, expense breakdowns, top clients) for data-driven decisions.

Supporting differentiators:

  1. True Net Earnings for Freelancers
     Unlike generic bookkeeping apps, KaisySales was designed around the gig

     breakdowns (Upwork vs Fiverr vs direct clients).

  2. Milestone-Based Project Billing
     Built-in support for deposits and milestone payments (e.g., "50%

     Retainers and subscriptions are tracked separately so owners always know
     their predictable monthly baseline versus one-off windfalls.

  4. All-in-One Simplicity
     Sales + Expenses + Inventory + Invoicing + Customers + Reports in ONE
     tool - eliminating the need for multiple disconnected apps, while
     staying dramatically simpler than traditional accounting software.

  5. Zero-Cost Entry
     Free to start with full access to all features - removing every barrier
     for the entrepreneurs who need financial clarity the most but can afford

  6. Local-First, Global-Ready
