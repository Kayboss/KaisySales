01 — Logo

The Leaf Mark & Wordmark

Our symbol is a sprouting leaf: growth rooted in nature. It reflects organic business growth and our earthy African heritage palette. The wordmark uses Manrope ExtraBold (900), sentence case — never all-caps.

Primary lockups

KaisySales
Horizontal · Light background
KaisySales
Reversed · Terracotta background
Mark only · Forest background

Rules of use

  • Clear space: keep empty space around the mark equal to at least the height of the letter “K” in the wordmark.
  • Minimum size: mark-only 20 px / 6 mm; lockup 110 px wide on screen.
  • App icon / favicon: leaf mark centered on Terracotta rounded square (radius ≈ 22% of size), white glyph.
  • Stroke weight: always 2 px stroke at 24 px viewBox — do not thin or bold the glyph.

Do

  • Use white or Terracotta for the glyph
  • Place on cream, white, terracotta, or forest backgrounds
  • Keep the leaf upright and unmodified

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, add shadows, or recolor to gradients
  • Place on busy photos without a scrim
  • Rearrange the lockup or change the font of the wordmark

03 — Typography

Manrope + Work Sans

Two Google Fonts, free and self-hostable. Manrope leads; Work Sans carries everything else.

Display / Headings — Manrope 700–900
Know your Business.

Weights: 400 · 600 · 700 · 800 · 900 — use 800–900 for page titles, 700 for card headings. Slight negative letter-spacing (-0.02em) at large sizes.

Body / UI — Work Sans 400–600
Record every transaction with ease. Track revenue, view daily summaries, and stay on top of your cash flow.

Weights: 400 body · 500 labels · 600 buttons/emphasis · 700 strong data. Line-height 1.6 for paragraphs.

Data / Figures — Work Sans, tabular numerals
GH₵ 12,480.50

Always render currency and metrics with tabular figures (font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums) so columns align.

04 — Voice & Tone

A Mentor, Not a Bank

KaisySales speaks like the smart, dependable friend who knows your numbers and tells you the truth — encouraging, direct, jargon-free.

TraitIt sounds likeNever like
Simple“Add your first sale in under two minutes.”“Initialize your ledger configuration.”
Encouraging“Nice — you're 3 sales ahead of last week.”“Performance remains suboptimal.”
Honest“GH₵230 went to platform fees this month.”Hiding costs or inflating numbers
Human“We'll remind you before anything changes.”Legalese in product UI
  • Sentence case everywhere — headlines, buttons, labels.
  • Money talks straight: show GH₵ amounts with two decimals.
  • Errors own the fix: say what happened and what to do next (“That password needs one number — try again”).
  • Celebrate the user's wins, never the software (“Your invoice is out!” not “KaisySales has generated invoice #204”).

06 — Applications

Where the Brand Lives

  • Product UI: cream canvas, white cards, terracotta actions, forest/red semantics, Recharts visualizations in the brand series.
  • Landing page: dark terracotta hero sections, gold accent underlines, laptop mockups framed in forest green.
  • Invoices (user-facing output): clean white sheet, terracotta header band optional, business logo slot, tabular totals.
  • Social & marketing: terracotta or forest backgrounds, gold quote marks for testimonials, leaf mark top-left, tagline bottom.
  • Email: single-column, white body on cream frame, terracotta CTA button, plain-text fallback friendly.
07 — Quick Reference

Brand Checklist

  • ✓ Leaf mark present, unmodified, correct clear space
  • ✓ Manrope for headings, Work Sans for body — nothing else
  • ✓ 60/30/10 color balance respected
  • ✓ Green = in, Red = out — semantics intact
  • ✓ Sentence case, jargon-free copy
  • ✓ 4px spacing grid, 8px radius defaults, warm shadows
  • ✓ Currency shown with tabular figures: GH₵0.00
  • ✓ Tagline when space allows: “Know your Business, Stay in Control.”
